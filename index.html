What do you need to get started?+

Access to your ad accounts through partner access, so you keep full ownership, plus your goal and your current numbers.

Do I pay you or the ad platform for ad spend?+

You pay TikTok, Meta or Google directly for ad spend. My fee covers set-up, management, optimization and reporting.

How do you report results?+

A short weekly report with spend, purchases, cost per purchase and ROAS, what I changed, what I learned, and one clear recommendation for the next week.

Which platforms do you work on?+

TikTok Ads and TikTok Shop, Meta Ads (including CPAS), Google Ads and X Ads. I am strongest on TikTok and Meta.

How soon will I see results?+

Tracking and the first small tests go live in the first week or two. A reliable trend usually shows after a few weeks, depending on budget and the product.
